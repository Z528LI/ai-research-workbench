# Selection and Comparison Pipelines

These pages open reviewed snapshots of the English Research Workbench. The schedules below describe when the underlying research runs. Each public page shows the dates of the evidence it presents.

## Stock Behavior and Peers

- **Sources** — Adjusted A-share daily prices, benchmark histories, and dated company, industry and peer group records.
- **Processing** — The on-demand study aligns observations to shared completed sessions, then compares stock returns with benchmarks and peer groups across periods and in rising and falling markets.
- **Validation** — Checks cover listing identity, date alignment and sample coverage. Sparse series stay unclassified. Group membership dates are shown separately from the return window.
- **Published view** — [Stock Behavior and Peers](https://ai-research-workbench-z528li-20261106.quaintpartridge1.chatgpt.site/beta/index.html?view=1&tab=dashboard) uses a browser chart view over the reviewed comparison outputs, with period and sector filters.

## Technical Selection and Backtests

- **Sources** — A dated multi-market shortlist, completed-session price and volume observations, and archived research snapshots.
- **Processing** — The scheduled morning workbench refresh validates the shortlist and groups candidates into trend continuation, reversal and limit-up follow-up. Historical comparisons and forward tracking use their own observation windows.
- **Validation** — Checks tie the shortlist and rendered report to their inputs and verify each market's session date. Historical backtests and optional supporting research retain their separate evidence status.
- **Published view** — [Technical Selection and Backtests](https://ai-research-workbench-z528li-20261106.quaintpartridge1.chatgpt.site/daily5/index.html?scope=legacy) renders the dated selection report and a history dashboard from the available snapshots, with separate backtest views.

## Thematic Research Library

- **Sources** — Archived thematic and company studies, with approved English translations where available.
- **Processing** — An on-demand catalog groups the reports by research topic and stage while retaining each original research date.
- **Validation** — Catalog checks tie each available translation to the exact archived report. The catalog distinguishes confirmed original dates from dates still awaiting verification.
- **Published view** — [Thematic Research Library](https://ai-research-workbench-z528li-20261106.quaintpartridge1.chatgpt.site/serenity/index.html) renders the catalog as topic groups linked to the reviewed reports, opening English editions where available.

## Theme Replay

- **Sources** — Dated market theme rankings, stock membership lists, market observations, and separately collected company news.
- **Processing** — The scheduled evening review groups stocks by theme, aligns market and news evidence, and builds a daily review and rolling history.
- **Validation** — Checks cover dates and listing identities, completion of the theme stages, and a separate completion check for the daily news input. Supplemental context retains its own evidence status.
- **Published view** — [Theme Replay](https://ai-research-workbench-z528li-20261106.quaintpartridge1.chatgpt.site/themes/index.html#overview) renders the overview, theme details and rolling observations from the dated theme-review outputs.

## Limit-up Research

- **Sources** — Dated limit-up statistics and reviewed stock-level event records across five A-share listing groups, with public announcements and news where available.
- **Processing** — The after-close workflow screens the five groups on eligible trading days, aligns records by listing and session, and organizes historical counts and stock-level event details.
- **Validation** — Checks cover the statistical cutoff, group eligibility, duplicate records and coverage. The review distinguishes aggregate counts, individually dated events and independently verified event explanations.
- **Published view** — [Limit-up Research](https://ai-research-workbench-z528li-20261106.quaintpartridge1.chatgpt.site/limit-features/index.html) binds the reviewed November 3, 2026 screening snapshot to interactive history charts and stock lookup.
